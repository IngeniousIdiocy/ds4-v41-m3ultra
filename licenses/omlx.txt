ds4's V4.1 Metal decode adapts three pieces of oMLX, https://github.com/jundot/omlx, as read at
v0.7.3 (commit 9de79bc39a01c1f3fec1e849903aaf0dd5478d7a):

- kernel_dsv41_attn_tile in metal/flash_attn.metal, from attn_scores and attn_pv in
  omlx/patches/deepseek_v41/decode_layer.py and decode_fused.py, rewritten in Metal for ds4's
  key cache, partials and reduce, with a verify block's rows added.
- ds4_mxfp4_codes in metal/moe.metal, the FP4 decode by shifts, after fp4_decode8 in
  omlx/patches/deepseek_v41/decode_moe.py.
- The row-block-major order of the routed expert grids for several decode rows
  (kernel_mul_mv_id_mxfp4_pair_swiglu_rows_rblock_v41 in metal/moe.metal), after the same
  file's decode. Only the order was taken, no code.
